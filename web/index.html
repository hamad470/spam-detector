The model runs entirely in your browser, so messages you check never leave your device. It's an 82 MB download the first time; after that it's cached.

Known weakness: genuine service messages (one-time codes, delivery updates, bank alerts) are often flagged, because the public training data has the scams that imitate them but almost none of the real ones. Why

Why

    Each word is scored by removing it and measuring how far the scam log-odds move. Red words pushed towards scam and green words pushed away from it.

      Built by Hamad Ur Rehman. Fine-tuned DistilRoBERTa, int8 ONNX, running on ONNX Runtime Web. Data: UCI SMS Spam Collection, Mishra & Soni SMS Phishing Dataset, ExAIS SMS, Enron-Spam, SpamAssassin and the Nazario phishing corpus. A demo, not a security product: never trust a text just because a model said it was safe.
