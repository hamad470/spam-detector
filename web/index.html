    Robustness is the share of scams still caught when 30% of words are obfuscated (leetspeak, look-alike characters, spacing and typos mixed together). The methodology, figures and error analysis are in the report.

    Built by Hamad Ur Rehman. Fine-tuned DistilRoBERTa, int8 ONNX, running on ONNX Runtime Web. Data: UCI SMS Spam Collection (2011) and Mishra & Soni SMS Phishing Dataset (2022), both CC BY 4.0. A demo, not a security product: never trust a text just because a model said it was safe.
